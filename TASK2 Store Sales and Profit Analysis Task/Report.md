# Store Performance Analysis Report

## 1. Summary of Findings
After analyzing the dataset, here are the key takeaways:

### Sales Trends
- There is a consistent upward growth in sales over the analyzed period.
- The busiest months generally show a historical peak towards the end of the year.

### Product Categories
- **Technology** is the highest-grossing category.
- **Furniture** shows high volume but lower profit margins in certain sub-categories.

### Profitability
- The **Consumer** segment is the most profitable.
- Some sub-categories, such as **Tables**, are currently operating at a loss, suggesting a need for price adjustments or cost-cutting.

## 2. Sales Forecasting
Using a Linear Regression model, we have projected sales for the next 6 months. The model indicates a steady growth rate, with a positive coefficient of approximately 902 per month.

## 3. Conclusion
To optimize performance, the store should:
1. Focus marketing efforts on the high-profit Consumer segment.
2. Investigate the supply chain for the "Tables" sub-category to turn it profitable.
3. Prepare inventory for the predicted growth in the coming months.
